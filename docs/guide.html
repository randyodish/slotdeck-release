User Guide

SlotDeck is a Windows DJ app for managing a west coast swing music library and playing it live.

The window

The window has three rows:

  • Toolbar (top): load or refresh the library, Automix, Audio Settings, theme, and UI scale.
  • Library (middle): your tracks, with the Automix queue on the right.
  • Decks (bottom): Deck A and Deck B, each with its own EQ and volume.

Load your library

  1. Click the load button in the toolbar and choose your music folder.
  2. SlotDeck scans it and lists your tracks, with a track count above the table.
  3. Added new music? Click refresh to rescan the same folder.

SlotDeck remembers your folder and settings and restores them next time you open the app.

Search and edit tracks

  • Search by title, artist, BPM (beats per minute, the tempo), key, genre, or energy.
  • Choose columns: right-click any column header to show or hide Title, Artist, Length, BPM, Key, Energy, and Genre. Drag column edges to resize.
  • Edit: change a track's title, artist, energy, or genre in the table, then click Save on that row. Length, BPM, and Key are read-only.

Set up audio outputs

Click Audio Settings in the toolbar. SlotDeck uses two separate outputs:

  • Master: what the audience hears. Decks A and B play here.
  • Headphones: what only you hear. Previews play here.

Each selector shows PASS or X to say whether that device works. Use the refresh control if you plug in a new device. While a deck or Automix is playing, the master selector and refresh are locked so the music can't be interrupted.

Preview a track

Preview lets you audition a song in your headphones before playing it for the room.

  1. Set a headphones output that shows PASS. Until then, Preview is disabled.
  2. Click Preview on a track. A progress meter appears on its row.
  3. Click Stop preview, or preview another track, to stop it.

Play on Deck A and B

  1. In a track's Action column, click A or B to load it onto that deck.
  2. Click Play / Pause on the deck. Both decks can play at once.
  3. Each deck shows elapsed, remaining, and total time.
  4. Click the waveform to jump to that spot, playing or paused.
  5. Use each deck's EQ (bass, mid, treble) and volume sliders to shape the sound.
  6. Click Remove to unload a deck.

Automix queue

Automix plays a list of songs back to back, so you don't have to load each one by hand.

  1. Click AM+ on a track to add it to the queue on the right.
  2. Drag to reorder, preview, or remove queued tracks.
  3. Choose which deck Automix uses (Deck A or B). This is locked while it runs.
  4. Click Start. It's enabled once a library is loaded, the queue has tracks, and the master output shows PASS.

If the chosen deck is idle, the first song starts right away. If it already has a song, Automix waits and starts the next one when that song ends. Click Stop to end Automix.

Playlists and folders

  • Create, rename, duplicate, delete, and export named playlists. Ctrl-click or Shift-click selects several for batch actions.
  • Open a playlist to use it like a smaller library: search, preview, load to a deck, add, remove, and reorder songs.
  • Send to Automix: append a playlist to the queue, or replace the queue with it (you'll be asked to confirm).
  • Import playlists from .m3u or .m3u8 files.
  • Folders group playlists one level deep. Drag playlists in, or use Move to folder.

History

SlotDeck records each set you play automatically. Open History in the Collections pane to review a set, export it to PDF, or delete it.

Themes and display

  • Theme: switch between night and day.
  • UI scale: make the interface larger or smaller from the toolbar.
  • Full screen: borderless full screen that covers the taskbar.

SlotDeck is built to keep playing smoothly while you use other apps on the same computer.
